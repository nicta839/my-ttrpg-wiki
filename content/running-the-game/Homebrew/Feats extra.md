I am thinking about introducing feats to accommodate for character concepts that you could come up with. If you want to make a double whip user or someone who has the "Monkey Grip" ability that allows you to wield a 2-handed weapon using one hand, we can talk about how to make that happen in the form of a Feat. Come talk to me about it and we can see what we can do.

There will be restrictions at the beginning and I'm looking for some willingness from you to work with me. I would prefer to have them "undertuned" and then tune them up rather than the other way around and nerfing you to the ground.
