## Map label audit

These visible labels are not mapped yet because they need a more confident article target or are too minor for the first public layer:

- Most named towns, inns, shrines, bridges, and adventure sites — useful later as settlement or point-of-interest pages.
- Sea and bay labels around the continent — better handled by a later map-feature pass.
- Small roads and route labels — left unmarked so the v1 map stays readable.
