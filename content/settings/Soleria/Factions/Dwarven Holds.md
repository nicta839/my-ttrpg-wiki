The dwarves, having regained adequate strength, have sent a small band of adventurers to pursue Ixis (Nerigoth's pet and originator of the Dwarven corruption), and set up an encampment on the western shores called Valor's Rest, willing to brave the danger of the Endless Maelstrom to end a centuries-old vendetta.
