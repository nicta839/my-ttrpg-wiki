There are several mysteries and lost knowledge that remain to be uncovered in the world

Mysteries of the world:

- Can the darkness be erased in Night's Reach?
- Who is Magnar and what are the Pyreborn?
- Can the Maelstrom be dispersed?
- Why did the Sunwell dim?
- Can the cursed suffered by the people in the Shattered land be removed?

Outstanding lost knowledge/treasure:

- Knowledge of spells used by Alera
- Celnaeus pieces
- Blackblade of Nerigoth, Pernicion
- The dwarven treasures of their "purged" halls
- Ixis' current state
- Location of the egg of Omael
- Ancestral knowledge of the elves
- The gnomes link to the gods
