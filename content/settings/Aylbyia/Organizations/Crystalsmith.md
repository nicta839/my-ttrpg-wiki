Honored reputation: Best artisans there are

Shadow reputation: Hiding some nefarious interest

Gain reputation by:

- Completing tasks for them
- Offering them gifts in the form of knowledge about stone

They are an organization made up exclusively of [[settings/Aylbyia/Society/Ancestries/Mountain|Mountain]] [[settings/Aylbyia/Society/Ancestries/Dwarves|Dwarves]]. Their headquarters is in the [[settings/Aylbyia/Settlements/Tower of a Thousand Stars|Tower of a Thousand Stars]] from where they ship their cargo. The cargo is escorted by one of their members until their location. They can be paid in goods or knowledge and lore about stone. They prefer to be paid upfront for their work. They stay mostly silent except for these rare trade transactions. They sometimes ask for people to procure them with rare goods and ingredients.
