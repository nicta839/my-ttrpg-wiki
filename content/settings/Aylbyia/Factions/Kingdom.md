Shadow Nature: Arrogance

Capital: [[settings/Aylbyia/Settlements/Ziebglen|Ziebglen]], Seat of [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Saints/Aumogonne|Aumogonne]]

Leader: King [[settings/Aylbyia/NPCs/kingdom/Johan de Meyer|Johan de Meyer]], old veteran of the battlefield. He was maimed during a defense of Klett and was since then forced into taking a more serious hand into politics.

War: Military tactics and the superior use of maneuvers to flank a more numerous army. Heavy use of traps and ambushes.

Commerce: Grain and other produce of the earth.

Politics: Currently controls the [[settings/Aylbyia/Regions/The Platsmoor|Platsmoor]]. The movement of the Orcish hordes is worrying.

Shadow activities: smuggling of goods and people. Kidnapping of dwarven craftsmiths, ransom.

The people have a normal lifespan in this parts of the world. Some say that the lineage of the king is not blessed like that of the [[settings/Aylbyia/Factions/Queendom|queendom]]. The throne is inherited by the eldest child of the regent. The rest of the country follows the matrilinear model.
