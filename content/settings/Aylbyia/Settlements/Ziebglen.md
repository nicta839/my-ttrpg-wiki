Most important city: Ziebglen, Seat of [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Saints/Aumogonne|Aumogonne]]

Named after Saint [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Saints/Aumogonne|Aumogonne]] who held back the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Betrayer|Betrayer]] in order for the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Celestials|Celestials]] to destroy Them.

Key Locations:

- Mines: Mines of [[settings/Aylbyia/Society/Redsteel|Redsteel]]
- Fountain of Life: Site of worship of the Church where knights go to clear themselves of impurities under the watchful eye of the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/The Planes/Heavens|Heavens]].
- Celestial Tower: Seat of Power of the City. It is a tower shaped castle that looms over the city. Inhabitants use its shadow to tell what time it is.
- Military Academy: Prestigious academy teaching warfare. These students then go onto getting into the church or are nobles who come for training.
