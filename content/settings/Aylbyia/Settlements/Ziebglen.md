Most important city: Ziebglen, Seat of Aumogonne

Named after Saint [[Aumogonne]] who held back the [[Betrayer]] in order for the [[Celestials]] to destroy Them.

Key Locations:

- Mines: Mines of [[Redsteel]]
- Fountain of Life: Site of worship of the Church where knights go to clear themselves of impurities under the watchful eye of the Heavens.
- Celestial Tower: Seat of Power of the City. It is a tower shaped castle that looms over the city. Inhabitants use its shadow to tell what time it is.
- Military Academy: Prestigious academy teaching warfare. These students then go onto getting into the church or are nobles who come for training.
