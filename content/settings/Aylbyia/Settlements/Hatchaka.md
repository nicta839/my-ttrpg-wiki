Most important city: Hatchaka

Isolated town connected to civilization only by the sea and its port. Hatchaka enjoys the peace of a small port town isolated from the world and independent. Their main export is the [[settings/Aylbyia/Society/Silksteel|Silksteel]] that is harvested from the dangerous spiders of the forest that lie to the North of it.

Key Locations:

- Market: On this market are traded fresh fish, [[settings/Aylbyia/Society/Silksteel|silksteel]] and other oddities of this faraway land. Some people even organize spider fights here.
- Harbor: The harbor is a small village of its own from where people board larger boats to go fish or trade with boats to large to make it upstream
