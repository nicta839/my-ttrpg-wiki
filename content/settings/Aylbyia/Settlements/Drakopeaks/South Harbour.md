Most important city: South Harbour

Only means of getting onto this part of the continent without going through the [[settings/Aylbyia/Regions/Twilight|Twilight]]. It is the stronghold of those who [[settings/Aylbyia/Factions/Churches/Worship of the Scaled Ones|worship the Scaled Ones]]. It is also the only place where one can get [[settings/Aylbyia/Society/Redsteel|Redsteel]].
