Most important city: Junon, The Celestial City

The Holy seat of the [[Church of the Flame]] and the heart of the [[Queendom]]. It is a sprawling city that spans along the Coast. All manner of goods can be found here. There is a saying "if you can't find what you look for in Junon, then you don't need it".

Important locations:

- Cathedral of the Subservient Worshipper: Grandest edifice of the church made of yellow marble. The architecture of this edifice allows for the sun to shine its light for the longest in the city.
- Airship yard: Closely protected construction secrets of the airships are guarded here. The ruckus of the yard indisposes the neighbors.
- Shipyard and docks: First place of entry for goods into the city. Most of the trading involving beasts and fish is done here.
- Titan Keep: A tall building made of green stone. It is thought to have been carved from the limb of some gigantic creature.
- Market of the Five Stars: This is the booming heart of the city. It is the place to find all things to trade legitimate or not. Stray too far from the main avenues to it and you will find the cutthroats and brothels.
