Most important city: Tavik Nurlowk

Reminiscnent in name to the Corrupted city of Tavik, this City was a fortified castle meant to stop the Orcish Warbands from coming into [[settings/Aylbyia/Factions/Queendom|Queendom]] territory. The City fell a year ago to the greatest host of [[settings/Aylbyia/Society/Ancestries/Orcs|Orcs]] and Goblins ever seen in living memory. Not much is known of its current situation at the moment.
