Most important city: Tavik Nurlowk

Reminiscnent in name to the Corrupted city of Tavik, this City was a fortified castle meant to stop the Orcish Warbands from coming into Queendom territory. The City fell a year ago to the greatest host of Orcs and Goblins ever seen in living memory. Not much is known of its current situation at the moment.
