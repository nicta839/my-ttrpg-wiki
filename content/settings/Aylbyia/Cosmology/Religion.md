Cycle of life and death:

Once someone dies, their soul vacates their body and travels to the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/The Planes/Astral Sea|Astral Sea]]. There on the "shore" they await to be attended to by the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Celestials|Celestials]]. If they are deemed ready, humble, pure and faithful, they are invited in the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/The Planes/Heavens|Heavens]] to experience eternal bliss and happiness and are absorbed into the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/The Planes/Primordial Fire|Primordial Fire]] that is the source of the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Celestials|Celestials]]' power. If they are deemed unfit, their soul is sent to a new body so that they may experience more growth throughout their next life.

As such necromancy is seen as a grave and terrible sin. Bringing people back from the dead is bad as it hinders their advancement through the cycles of rebirth. Not disposing of a body through cremation leads to their souls keeping a lingering attachment to something in the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/The Planes/Plane of Flesh|plane of flesh]]. Necromancy is a rejection of the values instilled by the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Celestials|Celestials]]. Extension of one's life through worship of [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/The Planes/Heavens|Heavens]] is seen as a blessing. It shows that [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/The Planes/Heavens|Heavens]] cares about you being on the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/The Planes/Plane of Flesh|Plane of Flesh]] and continuing to spread its message.

Only women may be part of the sanctionned clergy and only them have been known to hear the voices of the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Celestials|Celestials]] officially.
