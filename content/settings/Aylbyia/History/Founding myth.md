The [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/The Planes/Primordial Fire|Primordial Fire]] of the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/The Planes/Heavens|Heavens]] was revealed to the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/The Planes/Plane of Flesh|Plane of Flesh]] by the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Celestials|Celestials]]. Their influence brought the Tavik civilization up and enabled this world-sprawling empire to thrive and create wonders of magic. The [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/The Planes/Heavens|Heavens]] was the closest it had ever been to the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/The Planes/Plane of Flesh|plane of flesh]], with [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Celestials|Celestials]] walking the land and guiding the people.

The Celestial whose name was struck from history, was consumed by greed and betrayed the other [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Celestials|Celestials]]. The [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Betrayer|Betrayer]], as they would come to be known, wished for all power and domination over all that was on the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/The Planes/Plane of Flesh|Plane of Flesh]] and the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/The Planes/Heavens|Heavens]].

The [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Betrayer|Betrayer]] was fair, charismatic, powerful and scheming. The war that broke out for the very essence of all thinking beings and was devastating to the continent. The Tavik Empire was razed as a result. All races participated in the conflict with the exception of the [[settings/Aylbyia/Society/Ancestries/Elves|Elves]] (having not yet been seen on the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/The Planes/Plane of Flesh|Plane of Flesh]] until the return of the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Celestials|Celestials]] later). The [[settings/Aylbyia/Factions/Orcs and Goblinoids|Orcs and Goblinoids]] had sided with the forces of the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Betrayer|Betrayer]] as well as some [[settings/Aylbyia/Society/Ancestries/Humans|humans]] who defected from the Empire. The [[settings/Aylbyia/Society/Ancestries/Dwarves|Dwarves]] and remaining humans were pushed back by devious tactics and betrayals of close ones during the conflict.

The [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Betrayer|Betrayer]] slayed [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Cidias|Cidias]], Celestial of Harmony and Order and in the wake of the death of this divine being, the magic of the world was tainted by the gravest of sins. The pervasive flow of [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Arcane Magic|Arcane Magic]] emanating from the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/The Planes/Natural flow|Natural flow]] was thrown into chaos. No one would ever be able to wield [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Arcane Magic|arcane magic]] without taking the proper precautions.

The remaining [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Celestials|Celestials]] endowed a mortal with their powers while they performed a ritual to push the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/The Planes/Heavens|Heavens]] away from the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/The Planes/Plane of Flesh|Plane of Flesh]] and their envoy sealed the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Betrayer|Betrayer]] away. This mortal, named [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Saints/Junon|Junon]], met the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Betrayer|Betrayer]] on the fields of [[settings/Aylbyia/Regions/Twilight|Twilight]] and bought time for the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Celestials|Celestials]]to perform their ritual.

There, [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Saints/Junon|Junon]] died and burned her corporeal essence away to banish the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Betrayer|Betrayer]] for good. However, all was not won yet. Followers of the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Betrayer|Betrayer]] reached for [[settings/Aylbyia/Settlements/Ziebglen|Ziebglen]] in order to themselves disrupt the ritual and taint the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/The Planes/Heavens|Heavens]]. They were stopped by the great sacrifice of Saint [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Saints/Aumogonne|Aumogonne]] and her group of devout followers. The ritual was performed and the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Celestials|Celestials]] were not heard of for centuries. These [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Saints/Saints|Saints]] gave away freely their lives for the safety of all. They ascended as [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Celestials|Celestials]] in the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/The Planes/Heavens|Heavens]].

The Tavik empire crumbled, knowledge was lost and the rebuilding of civilization was difficult to come about. However 400 years ago, the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Celestials|Celestials]] re-established contact with the plane of flesh. They were here to redeem all of us and guide us to the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/The Planes/Heavens|Heavens]]. By "burning" their voices into the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/The Planes/Celestial flow|celestial flow]], they are able to speak with a select number of us and guide us. Thus we have grown, rebuilt and learned things that were long forgotten. We are more mindful of our place and serve the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Celestials|Celestials]] once more so that we may not be tempted once again by another [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Betrayer|Betrayer]].

- As taught by the Holy Prayer of the [[settings/Aylbyia/Factions/Churches/Church of the Flame|Church of the Flame]] of the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Celestials|Celestials]]
