 Sinderealms

- Sinder: Seat of power of House Sinder. It boasts a large harbour from which large freight ships leave.
- Frostmoot: Birthplace of the Shadowhand. This black rock castle in snow is rarely seen by the inhabitants of the region. It is supplied however regularly by house Sinder.
- Heartsdale: Lesser known temple of the church
- Neibenpoint: Contract and bond keepers for ships sailing outside of the baronnies.
- Glywoods: Home of bandits and cutthroats
