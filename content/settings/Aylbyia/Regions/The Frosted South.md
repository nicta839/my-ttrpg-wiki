Region Flavor: Frozen land and earth. Inhospitable but home to creatures that can bring a pretty penny

Geography: Tall mountains and frozen waters. It is blasted by cold and dry wind

Clothing: Layers upon layers of clothing to keep the cold and wind at bay. Lots of furs.

Art: Fishbone sculptures and little mechanical puzzles.

Food: Fish and seal meat

Lifestyle: Oriented towards fishing and trading with those who come here to seek fortune in exchange of shelter and material for their expedition.
