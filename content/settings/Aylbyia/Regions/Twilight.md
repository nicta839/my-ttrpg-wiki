The area that was said to have been the final battlefield of the war waged against the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/The Planes/Heavens|Heavens]] by the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Betrayer|Betrayer]]. It is an area from which nothing grows and that corrupts all those who live inside it. The people living at its border eventually turn into [[settings/Aylbyia/Society/Ancestries/Tiefling|tieflings]].

- [[settings/Aylbyia/Regions/Map Features/The Unholy Sepulcher|The Unholy Sepulcher]]: Said to be the location of the tomb for the body of the being who [[settings/Aylbyia/Factions/Shaïdin|betrayed]] the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/The Planes/Heavens|Heavens]].

- [[settings/Aylbyia/Regions/Map Features/The Hanging Tree|The Hanging Tree]]: Rumored to be an enormous tree that floats above the ground, roots reaching towards the sky. From the branches hang innumerable humanoids.

- [[settings/Aylbyia/Regions/Map Features/The Smoulderspine|The Smoulderspine]]: Tall volcano that is said to the the place from which the spirit of the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Betrayer|Betrayer]] was banished. Wounding the world and leaving the blight behind as a final spiteful action.

- Temple of Eternal Cinder: Temple of those who worshipped the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Betrayer|Betrayer]], ancient great city of the Tavik Empire.
