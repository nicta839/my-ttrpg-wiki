- Year 0

- The [[Celestials]] contact the [[Plane of Flesh]] and offer redemption
- First sighting of [[Halfling]] and [[Gnome]] who spread the word of the [[Celestials]]
- All races are touched by their call and send missionaries to convert the populace
- Castles are built at the edge of [[Twilight]]
- A new Empire is created with a seat in [[Ziebglen]]: Empress Dinara takes power over the Church and politics
- The [[Golden Mask]] is created

- Year 100

- The Orcs are pushed back to the [[Orkunsteppes]]
- Frostmoot and the [[Shadowhand]] are created
- The Empress dies mysteriously
- The succession war starts between the siblings and vassals
- A great earthquake is felt, the black mountains take their name as all vegetation dies on that mountain.

- Year 120

- Dragons and giants are released into the world and sprout out from [[The Dracopeaks]]. They wreak havoc on the land.
- The Dwarven clans of the Mountain and the Hillls split as the latter establish themselves near lake Koral
- The Church splits into Eastern and Western churches as factions start to align themselves with each of them.
- The Queendom is established in [[Junon, The Celestial City]] and [[The Platsmoor]] fall under the regency of the Church of the East.
- The Dragons disappear and giants are seen in [[The Frosted South]].

- Year 200

- The [[Queendom]] pushes its borders until Lake Koral and [[The Realm of Vuuch]]
- The [[Kingdom]] is born but the legitimacy of a male heir is disputed
- The King purges the church of the East and renames it [[Church of Light]]
- The [[Blue Council]] is created
-  [[settings/Aylbyia/Factions/The Great Houses of Humanity|The Great Houses of Humanity]] enter a tacit truce with the moderation of the [[Blue Council]]
- [The Shame made flesh](Shaïdin.md) appear for the first time

- Year 204

- The forces of Light are pushed back from deep within the [[Twilight]] to Blacksand Keep and [[Castle Bleakunpine]].
- Emergency aid and soldiers are sent to keep the [Shame made flesh](Shaïdin.md) from overwhelming the continent.
- The [[Band of Blades]] is created
- The king in [[The Platsmoor]] uses this opportunity to retake Lake Koral and Hachenford from the [[Queendom]].
- [Twilight worshippers](Shaïdin.md) and [tieflings](Tiefling.md) are seen for the first time.
- [[Elves]] come forth from [[Fin's End]]. Watchtowers are built

- Year 230

- The threat from the [[Twilight]] fades
- House Coral's leader is found murdered
- House Overstar and House Coral enter war
- A boat from house Sinder is sank by an unknown vessel
- House Sinder wages war against house Coral
- The House of Water enters an alliance with House Coral and pushes back against House Sinder.
- The blessed griffons are found in Hallowroost
- The [[Delvers]] organization is created
- [[Tiulasa]] colonies are found once more

- Year 270

- The war of the Houses comes to an end
- The Tavik ruins are once more operational for the most part
- [[Tiulasa]] colonies are rebuilt.
- All contact with [[The Dracopeaks]] is lost.
- An elven colony is established in [[Tritonya]]
- An expedition to [[The Eastern Continent]] finds the people coming back with their memories wiped

- Year 300

- The Queen of Skal and the King of the Platsmoor meet for the first time in 100 years
- The first [[Dragonborn]] are seen in [[South Harbour]]
- [[Tritonya]] independence is denied and its populace is scattered throughout the [[Queendom]]
- A series of assassinations of [[Blue Council]] members occur and throws the organization into disarray
- A colony is created [[South-of South Harbour]] for the exploration of that landmass.

- Year 320

- The world is now aware of the[[Scaled Ones]] but largely misunderstood.
- Veterans of the [[Twilight]] turned [[Tiefling]] are given a retirement location in Faindale.
- [[Dragonborn]] establish themselves in the [Baronnies](settings/Aylbyia/Factions/The%20Great%20Houses%20of%20Humanity.md).
- The [[Mountain]] dwarves make themselves known to the world by creating the [[Tower of a Thousand Stars]] and the [[Crystalsmith]].
- The [[Blue Council]] finally recovers and resumes its activities as a bank and diplomat organisation.
- An expedition to [[The Eastern Continent]] fails to come back
- The Queen is deemed to follow heretical beliefs and sentenced to death. Her younger sister comes to power.

- Year 360

- The Smoulderspine becomes active once more
- The [[Band of Blades]] and the [[Golden Mask]] thread into [[Twilight]] in a preventive strike
- The army disappears into the [[Twilight]].
- The kingdom is heavily involved in the defense of Klett from Hobgoblins. The king is maimed in the final push
- Several Baronesses are found murdered.
- War breaks out between the Houses
- Frostmoot goes silent

- Year 387

- Anti-church sentiment grows in Faindale
- [[Shadowhand]] agents are seen working in groups
- The [Baronnies](settings/Aylbyia/Factions/The%20Great%20Houses%20of%20Humanity.md) are financially broke, they are bailed out by [[The Coin]]
- The War of the 4 Houses ends, the [[Band of Blades]] is nearly destroyed by the conflict
- The Eastern Church's influence grows towards the baronnies, supported by the mountain dwarves' patronage
- First flight of an airship escorted by griffons over [[Junon, The Celestial City]].

- Year 392

- One shot: Looming danger
- The [[Sparrow Trading Company]] is created as airship travel starts to take root for trade and travel.
- Tensions are at a boil in Faindale as a tiefling kid is found severely injured after an altercation with [[Golden Mask]] members
- The kingdom amasses troop in Hachenford
- Portents of a great conflict are prophesized by [[Maeva Delmin]] (Kingdom high cleric)
- [[Tavik Nurlowk]] and Farshore fall to the [[Orcs and Goblinoids]] hordes.
- Refugees arrive in Pelcow and Koral City
- The Queen dies and is succeeded by her daughter Queen Nahla

- Year 393

- The Queen extends a hand of friendship towards the People of Faindale
- [[The Coin]] is under pressure from trade moving through airships
- The [[Kingdom]] moves its troops north towards Koral city and Klett
- Tensions in Faindale start to lessen
- The number of [[Elves]] leaving Fin's End increases. They are sent mostly to the castles bordering the [[Twilight]].

- Year 394

- An expedition into the [[Orkunsteppes]] does not come back
- The queen and her family go to Faindale for a peace summit
- The [[Kingdom]] organizes raids to find the [[Orcs and Goblinoids]]
- The Estate of the Queen burns and the royal family is found murdered, except for the daughter
- Local radical secessionists are accused
- Violent repression ensues

- Year 395

- Revolution erupts in Faindale
- The [[Golden Mask]] and [[Band of Blades]] are called to cull the uprising
- The Wager falls to the revolutionary forces
- [[The Realm of Vuuch]] is inhabited by Orcish colonies
- Goblinoids and orcs are seen on the other side of the Haerone river
- One shot: Early warnings. 

- Year 396

- The Wager is taken back by the forces of the Queendom
- Faindale is under siege
