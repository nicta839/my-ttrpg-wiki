They operate as a middle stage in the cycle of rebirth. Gifted in crafts. Less attached to their physical mortal body than the [[Dwarves]] in the cycle. Their races announced the return of the [[Celestials]]. Very religious backgrounds as the messengers of the return of the [[Celestials]].
