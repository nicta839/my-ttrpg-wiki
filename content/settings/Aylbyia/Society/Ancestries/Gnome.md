They operate as a middle stage in the cycle of rebirth. Gifted in crafts. Less attached to their physical mortal body than the [[settings/Aylbyia/Society/Ancestries/Dwarves|Dwarves]] in the cycle. Their races announced the return of the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Celestials|Celestials]]. Very religious backgrounds as the messengers of the return of the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Celestials|Celestials]].
