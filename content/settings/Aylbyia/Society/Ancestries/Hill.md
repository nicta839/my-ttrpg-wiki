They are less stocky than their compatriotes. These [[settings/Aylbyia/Society/Ancestries/Dwarves|dwarves]] live mostly in the valley East of [[settings/Aylbyia/Regions/The Younger Brother|the Younger Brother]], they are thought to be greedy and hoarding lots of money by other races. They are usually capable of great craftsmanship.
