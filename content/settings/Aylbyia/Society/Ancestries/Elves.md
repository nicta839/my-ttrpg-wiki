Recent additions to the cycle or rebirth. They are seen as "cheating" because of their innate affinity to magic without having taken part to the clash that destroyed the [[settings/Aylbyia/Cosmology/Celestials and Saints/Celestials/Betrayer|Betrayer]] as well as their long lifespan that is not bestowed by the Church. They are invariably unaware of what is going on in [[settings/Aylbyia/Regions/Fin's End|Fin's End]], their memories have been wiped. They have unmistakable extremely long and pointy ears that are impossible to hide unless wearing uncomfortable headgear.

Some elves start exhibiting violent tendencies at a certain age. When this happens, a [[settings/Aylbyia/Society/Ancestries/Shadar-kai|Shadar-kai]] or a [[settings/Aylbyia/Society/Ancestries/Drow|Drow]] will come collect the child for training and enrollment amongst the [[settings/Aylbyia/Organizations/Shadowhand|Shadowhand]] against compensation. This deal is apparently well tolerated by the various large factions that welcome [[settings/Aylbyia/Society/Ancestries/Elves|Elves]] in their midst
