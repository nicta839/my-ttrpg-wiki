Redsteel is mined and made into ingots through unknown means in [[settings/Aylbyia/Regions/The Dracopeaks|the Dracopeaks]] and in [[settings/Aylbyia/Settlements/Ziebglen|Ziebglen]]. This formidable metal is able to withstand incredible temperatures, can only be metled using magical fire and resists most of any kind of punishment. This metal is sometimes used as coating on bolts or arrowheads in order to knock out stone defenses. These missiles fetch a very good price and are recovered after battles. Weapons are forged from this metal and more rarely armor. Magical enchantment of this metal has so far been impossible.
