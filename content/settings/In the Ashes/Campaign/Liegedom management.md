This system should not distract from the characters being central.

Projects: Long term endeavors that will require multiple checks be made in order to have them accomplished.

Events: Momentus things that happen in the Liegedom and that need to be fixed otherwise it'll come at the price of a penalty.

Opportunities: Momentus things that grant you a benefit if succeeded.

The goal or mechanic to build here is that not everything can be done at the same time.
