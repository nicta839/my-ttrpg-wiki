Communities build around stones called Homestones. Homestones run the gambit from skipping pebble to boulder. They burn anyone but their rightful "ruler" and only one person may be attuned to a stone, but a person may be attuned to many stones.

Homestones help protect a community against XXXXXX in a certain radius. The homestones also have different magical properties depending on the wielder. The potency of a homestone is proportional to the strength of the will of the wielder as well as the amount of people who swear fealty to them.
