Kingdom

The restrictions to each of these areas help you understand the flavor of what I want to get from them.

Liege: King or Queen, you take the decisions (anyone)

Prime Minister/Administrator: Make the Liege’s decision happen (anyone)

Arcane Advisor: Advises on matters of magic (can cast a spell or has proficiency in arcana)

War Primarch: Advises on matters of war (Martial class or proficiency in History)

Whisperer: Advises on information (Rogue or Bard, or proficiency in Insight, Investigation, History or Stealth)

The Coin: Administers the gold that goes around (Anyone who has a proficiency in persuasion, insight or has a background linked to Merchants)

Conduit: Listens to the spirits and advises on matters of the soul (paladins, clerics, druid and warlocks)

Lorekeeper: Head diplomat for the kingdom (Bards, or proficiency in Persuasion, Deception, History or Intimidation)
