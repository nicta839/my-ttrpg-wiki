Old bearded gnomish woman who knew of [[settings/Tales of Fate/Campaign/Players/Kissa|Kissa]]'s grandmother. She lives in a mushroom in a cave some ways away from [[settings/Tales of Fate/Settlements/Pelfort|Pelfort]].

Grey blue hair with cool tattoos on her arms that are intertwined shapes of animals. She was very good at making fake magic tricks. She is an experienced tinkerer.

Kissa predicted that she would save the last unicorn but that her family will perish when she did so.
