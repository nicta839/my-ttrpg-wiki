The [[settings/Tales of Fate/Organizations/Delian Order|Delian Order]] fought death and served life. They hid the trinity artifacts from the world and disappeared.

800 years ago Ueli was kicked out of the Great Wizard council

50 years ago [[settings/Tales of Fate/Campaign/Players/Noita|Noita]] studied under him

The world has known Crusades against areas where religions other than the one professed by the Church of the Spiral are worshipped.

There was an ancient conflict between wizards and a thieve's guild. [[settings/Tales of Fate/Campaign/Players/Kissa|Kissa]]'s grandmother was part of it.

It was decided by the [[settings/Tales of Fate/Organizations/Council of Great Wizards|Council of Great Wizards]] that some forms of magic be outlawed if practiced away from licensed institutions and practitioners.
