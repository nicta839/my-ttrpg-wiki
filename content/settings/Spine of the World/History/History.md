- -5000
- Arrival of the Elves on the Prime Material
- Elves mercilessly push the Orcs from the land
- Elves start waging war on the Human Kingdom of Salidar and other human settlements
- Elves push this Kingdom to its capital but can't take it without triggering the Kingdom's secret weapon.
- The Knights of the Frost Vale protecting the Tree of Summer are called to defend what is left of the Kingdom and push back the Elven forces
- A close advisor to Shuraz decides to play double agent and proposes to allow a possibility for the Kingdom to take revenge on the Empire at a later date.
- Shuraz conjures and binds Death itself to battle this secret weapon
- The kingdom's aristocracy is cursed to immortality and forgetting until Death and the weapon are released from their binding. Only parts of this curse function.
- Construction of Ebou Dar starts. The Circuits are built
- Kuldahar is reduced to a handful of houses, the druid is replaced by an elven keeper.
- The Frost Vale is collapsed and its surviving inhabitants cursed.
- -2000
- Shuraz retreats to the Feywild once the Empire is established
- The Dwarves fall under the regency of the Empire but can worship their own pantheon as long as they pay heavy taxes.
- Tar Valon is built
- Discovery of the Craddle of Moradin
- -1000
- -500
- -150
- Hrothgar escapes the dwarven holds and works in the Empire as an assistant to a merchant
- Creation of Uke by Orrik's mentor (an elf) and transformation of his partner.
- -100
- Murder of the last Queen of Ummdrumkaz'ad and end of the Stonefang clan lineage.
- Disappearance of msot Eladrin from the Prime Material.
- First gnome to enter the Academy for Magic of Tar Valon as a student.
- First Dwarven establishment of trade is created top-side. It will become the Whisper Road
- -50
- Official end of slavery
- Anti-empire sentiments are raised in the North with people running towards the forest.
- The Grey Glove establishes itself as a parallel market for dubiously acquired goods from recently deceased individuals in Whitebridge.
- The Whisper Road grows to create a secure framework from which non-elves can work
- Military intervention is used to curtail the various uprisings.
- -20
- Ris'san Dameno is created and start raising trouble in the Northernmost parts of the Empire
- Settlements are raided
- The Grey Glove expands its influence rapidly
- Mercenaries are hired to handle the raids as the armies of the Empire are too few to cover all the ground
- Umdrummkaz'ad is lost to elementals from the depths aided by dark elves. The dwarves find refuge in Rakkar.
- 0 (Start of Campaign)
