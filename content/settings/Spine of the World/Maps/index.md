## Map label audit

These visible labels are not mapped yet because they need a more confident campaign article target:

- Named nations and regions not yet represented in the vault.
- Minor city labels outside the campaign’s current public notes.
- Route annotations and handwritten travel marks, which are better left as visual map context for now.
