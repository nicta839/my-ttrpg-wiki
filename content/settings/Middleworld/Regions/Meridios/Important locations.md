Melvern: Location of the third obelisk, village of devout eladrins.

Gallaran: Main port of arrival to this part of the world and location of the first obelisk.

Eremir: Location of the second obelisk, warmer climates offer the possibility of growing cereals.

Saeras: Location of the 4th obelisk, this deep green forest is the home of small druidic communities.
