The Gods exist in a dimension/plane of their own making. It is cut mostly from mortals through a Veil. This can only be crossed by those who die or seen by Humans in their dreams.

# Old Gods:

Lord of the Grave: (Domain: Grave/knowledge/Death) He looks over the proper treatment of corpses and collects the knowledge accumulated during one's life. Through death, nothing is lost; it is simply another step into the journey from nothingness to nothingness. He is symbolized by a skeletal palm with an eye in its center.

Waits in Shadow: (Domain: Trickery/War) She is the one who pushes gingerly the winds so that the incriminating letter may fall into the right hand for conflict to arise. She is both seen as good and bad luck. Bad luck often comes peeking its head in the form of bloodshed. She is symbolized by a simple featured smiling mask.

The Inevitable: (Domain: Law/Order/Fate) What is meant to come will come. They are the one who inspires those who go seek wrongdoers to bring them to face the consequences of their actions. They are symbolized by crossed sword and a velum scroll.

# New Gods:

Dolene: (Domain: Forge/Merchants) Patron of those who craft and merchants. She favors those who are willing to put in the hard work in order to succeed in their endeavors. She is symbolized by a coin

Agarion: (Domain: Life) Celebrate the coming of dawn and spring as life comes anew in the world. He inspires those who protect life in all its forms. He is symbolized by a dawning sun.

Fibax: (Domain: Light) She shines the way for those who thread the world by day, who need protection in darkness and those who pass in order for them to cross the Veil. She is symbolized by a lantern with blue fire.

Walkov: (Domain: Nature/ Tempest) He keeps the land fertile and wild, brings the needed rain and the unwelcomed storm. Respected by those who work the land and feed from it. He is symbolized by a tree on fire.

Hildaria: (Domain: Redemption) Those who cannot find the way are not lost. Those in prison, those who heal soldiers from either side after the battle call upon Hildaria for guidance and to find a purpose to turn things around. She does not carry any particular symbol as anyone may worship her in their own way.

Salena: (Domain: Magic) The Goddess of Magic was slayed and caused the fall of Elderon in a magical storm and miasma. Although she held the domain of magic, it is still accessible today and the mana wells seem to be a geological feature of the world. It is thought that she could control all the mana winds and that they are now free to blow through the world. She is/was symbolized by large waves. She still has worshippers across the world.
