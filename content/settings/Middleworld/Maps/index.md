## Map label audit

These visible labels are not mapped yet because they either need a clearer article target or are too local for the first world-map layer:

- City names and smaller town labels across the main continent — need settlement article decisions.
- Ocean, sea, mountain-range, and channel labels — useful as map features later, but lower priority than inhabited regions.
- Isolated islands such as Alba and the Sunken Isle — need lore/article confirmation before becoming public markers.
