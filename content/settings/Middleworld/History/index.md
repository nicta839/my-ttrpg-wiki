- -500
  - [[settings/Middleworld/Factions/Vallios|Vallios]] rediscovered and rebuilt by Western Factions
- -300
  - [[settings/Middleworld/Regions/Meridios|Meridios]] lands in the North are colonized by devout worshippers of the Lord of the Grave and Salena
  - Mapping of the North
  - Rise of Lura, the Godslayer
- 0
  - Cataclysm of [[settings/Middleworld/Regions/Elderon|Elderon]] and slaying of Magic Goddess Salena
  - Monstrosities spawn from the fallout
- 50
  - Creation of the [[settings/Middleworld/Organizations/Hunters|Hunters]]
  - Discovery of the Frostwell
  - [[settings/Middleworld/Regions/The Undersea|The Undersea]] folk come to be known to the world
- 100
  - Spreading of [[settings/Middleworld/Society/The Empire|the Empire]]
  - Ilorains fall to the Empire
  - The [[settings/Middleworld/Factions/Varesh|Varesh]] map the south of the continent and the outskirts of the Jungle to the West
  - Vallios becomes the seat of diplomatic exchange and negotiations
- 200
  - Great battles of [[settings/Middleworld/Regions/The Singing Plains|the Singing plains]]
  - Iskara and Vesna become Empire territory
  - A great alliance of the Western Factions is created to counter the influence of the Empire
  - The port of Vallios becomes the greatest in all the lands
- 300
  - The Empire push into [[settings/Middleworld/Factions/Gildain|Gildain]]
  - Great expeditions are sent from the Empire throughout the world to try and find new resources to exploit
  - [[settings/Middleworld/Regions/The Narthos|The Narthos]] is rediscovered and its spreading documented
  - Vallios establishes itself as an autonomous entity ruled by an elected council from the various districts and guilds that make it up
  - The Elven folk population is split between the Nevergreen Forest and the Forest of Veilin
  - Teria becomes Empire territory
- 350
  - Great lycan incident
  - The Lands south of the Skyreach Mountains are mapped in broad strokes
  - [[settings/Middleworld/Regions/Dolan|Dolan]] and [[settings/Middleworld/Regions/Rovena|Rovena]] are empire teritorries
- 400
  - [[settings/Middleworld/Factions/The Sovereign Cities|The Sovereign cities]] split from the Empire
  - The Varesh intervene and control the Southpass and Scarlet Seas
  - The Empire retreats to its controlled territories
- 450
  - Great sea battles are won by the Empire who now control the southern seas and snatch [[settings/Middleworld/Regions/Surnia And Numitorem|Surnia and Numitorem]]
  - The Empire is stretched to its limits
  - [[settings/Middleworld/Regions/Bay Of Skulls|Bay of Skulls]] pirates start encoraching upon Rovena coasts
- 500
  - Peace treaty between all great powers is signed in Vallios
  - Vallios welcomes representative of other nations' interests to have a seat at their council
  - Prisoners of war, convicts and other criminals are sent to [[settings/Middleworld/Regions/Ursa|Ursa]] to mine for mana crystals
- 550
  - Campaign Start
