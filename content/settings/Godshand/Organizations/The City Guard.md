Godshand's city guard investigates local crime and maintains a station in [[settings/Godshand/Settlements/Godshand - The Town|the town]]. The guard's work is documented most clearly in [[settings/Godshand/Campaign/Quests/CSI godshand|CSI Godshand]].

## Known personnel

[[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Verick|Verick]], [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Arti|Arti]], and [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Gill|Gill]] are three named investigators. Ferrin is a new guard posted at the crime scene where [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Aldrich|Aldrich]] has been found dead.

## The investigation

The case follows witness statements, a planned riverbank meeting, financial records, gambling debts, and medicine purchases. [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Hilda|Hilda Mirin]] supplies gossip and information about betting around [[settings/Godshand/Society/The Hand Debate|the Hand Debate]].

When [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Marla Greenleaf|Marla Greenleaf]] attacks the investigators with druidic magic and animated blights, Gill subdues her. The guards examine her notes and take her back to the station. Only one apothecary assistant is recorded as knowing about her detention apart from the guards.

## Authority and structure

The account establishes crime scene duty, investigation, and custody. It does not describe the guard's command structure, individual ranks, or its formal relationship with the magistrate and council.
