Crimp Padfoot is Andy's Divine Soul sorcerer in the Godshand campaign. He adventures with [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/RoBeert|RoBeert]], [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Peter Copperpot|Peter Copperpot]], and [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Liana|Liana]].

## Wild Sheep Chase

Crimp helps [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Shinebright|Shinebright]] return from sheep form during [[settings/Godshand/Campaign/Quests/wildsheep chase|Wild Sheep Chase]]. The party receives money and other compensation; the following adventure records Crimp gaining a +1d4 bonus to interactions with mages on mage business.

## The missing corn shipment

During [[settings/Godshand/Campaign/Quests/The Great Popcorn disappearance|The Great Popcorn Disappearance]], Crimp purchases an ox named Cherryblossom. The ox breaks through a locked entrance at [[settings/Godshand/Settlements/The McReady Farm|the McReady farm]]. Inside, Crimp notices that the symbol of [[settings/Godshand/Cosmology/The Jaded One|the Jaded One]] is upside down. Mentioning the family's daughters helps wake Diane from her trance.

He takes part in the party's investigation of the corrupted farm and its effort to stop the ritual in the cornfield.
