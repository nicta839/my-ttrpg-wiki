Gus survives the events of [[settings/Godshand/Campaign/Quests/wildsheep chase|Wild Sheep Chase]]. The [[settings/Godshand/Timeline|campaign timeline]] identifies Gus and [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Noke|Noke]] as the people who committed the crime associated with [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Shinebright|Shinebright]]'s transformation into a sheep.

## Recorded outcome

The adventuring party restores Shinebright to elven form, and both Gus and Noke remain alive at the end of the adventure. The local campaign account does not record Gus's later whereabouts, motives, or relationship with Noke in more detail.
