Liana is Alex's human druid in the Godshand campaign. She adventures with [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Crimp Padfoot|Crimp Padfoot]], [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/RoBeert|RoBeert]], and [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Peter Copperpot|Peter Copperpot]].

## Wild Sheep Chase

Liana helps restore [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Shinebright|Shinebright]] to elven form in [[settings/Godshand/Campaign/Quests/wildsheep chase|Wild Sheep Chase]]. The opening of the next adventure records her receiving four firebreathing potions, with a Constitution saving throw required.

## The corn farm

When [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Janos|Janos]] asks the party to investigate a missing corn shipment, Liana joins the journey to [[settings/Godshand/Settlements/The McReady Farm|the McReady farm]]. While crossing the cornfield, she heals a horse that then rushes back toward town.

She takes part in the investigation and the confrontation with the summoned shadow demon in [[settings/Godshand/Campaign/Quests/The Great Popcorn disappearance|The Great Popcorn Disappearance]].
