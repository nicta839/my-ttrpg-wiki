Arti is one of the three investigators in [[settings/Godshand/Organizations/The City Guard|Godshand's city guard]] named in [[settings/Godshand/Campaign/Quests/CSI godshand|CSI Godshand]], alongside [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Verick|Verick]] and [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Gill|Gill]].

## Investigation

The team investigates [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Aldrich|Aldrich]]'s death in the weeks around [[settings/Godshand/Society/The Hand Debate|the Hand Debate]]. Their inquiries connect the innkeeper's financial problems with witness testimony, controlled medicine purchases, and [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Marla Greenleaf|Marla Greenleaf]]'s work at the apothecary.

The session notes identify Arti as an investigator but do not give an individual specialty, rank, or personal history.
