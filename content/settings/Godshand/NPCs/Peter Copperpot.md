Peter Copperpot is William's dwarf monk, with Magic Initiate, in the Godshand campaign. He adventures with [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Crimp Padfoot|Crimp Padfoot]], [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/RoBeert|RoBeert]], and [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Liana|Liana]].

## Wild Sheep Chase

Peter helps rescue [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Shinebright|Shinebright]] during [[settings/Godshand/Campaign/Quests/wildsheep chase|Wild Sheep Chase]]. Alongside the party's shared compensation, he seeks a possible future favor from the wizard.

## Seeing through the farmhouse illusion

During [[settings/Godshand/Campaign/Quests/The Great Popcorn disappearance|The Great Popcorn Disappearance]], Peter hears breathing beyond the kitchen at [[settings/Godshand/Settlements/The McReady Farm|the McReady farm]]. This breaks the dining area's illusion for him, and he alerts his companions.

The discovery leads into the rescue of the twins from the storm cellar and the investigation of the farm's altered animals, [[settings/Godshand/Mysteries/Black Goo and Cornfield Corruption|black goo]], and ritual.
