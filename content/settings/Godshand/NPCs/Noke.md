Noke survives the events of [[settings/Godshand/Campaign/Quests/wildsheep chase|Wild Sheep Chase]]. The [[settings/Godshand/Timeline|campaign timeline]] names Noke and [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Gus|Gus]] as having committed the crime associated with [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Shinebright|Shinebright]]'s transformation into a sheep.

## Recorded outcome

Shinebright is returned to elven form by the party. Noke is still alive afterward, but the local notes do not establish his subsequent fate, motives, or any further confrontation with the rescued wizard.
