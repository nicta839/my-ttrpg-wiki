Verick is one of the three investigators in [[settings/Godshand/Organizations/The City Guard|Godshand's city guard]] named in [[settings/Godshand/Campaign/Quests/CSI godshand|CSI Godshand]], alongside [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Arti|Arti]] and [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Gill|Gill]].

## Investigation

The case concerns the death of [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Aldrich|Aldrich]], an innkeeper and judge for [[settings/Godshand/Society/The Hand Debate|the Hand Debate]]. It leads the investigators through witness statements, gambling debts, the apothecary trade, and the secrecy surrounding the judging panel.

Verick's individual actions, rank, and background are not recorded separately in the session account.
