RoBeert is Olli's human Champion in the Godshand campaign. He adventures with [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Crimp Padfoot|Crimp Padfoot]], [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Peter Copperpot|Peter Copperpot]], and [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Liana|Liana]]. The session notes also spell his name Robeert.

## Wild Sheep Chase

RoBeert is part of the party that restores [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Shinebright|Shinebright]] to elven form during [[settings/Godshand/Campaign/Quests/wildsheep chase|Wild Sheep Chase]].

## The cornfield vision

After the party frees Ruffles from the controlling crystals at [[settings/Godshand/Settlements/The McReady Farm|the McReady farm]], RoBeert tries to destroy the strange metal corn object expelled by the pig. A vision reveals a ritual in the cornfield and suggests a connection to the Hand and something beneath [[settings/Godshand/Regions/The Lake and Waterfall|the lake and mountain]].

His mind withdraws before the entity responsible can see him, and he heads toward the ritual. The party follows and ultimately defeats the summoned shadow demon. The vision is a clue to [[settings/Godshand/Mysteries/Black Goo and Cornfield Corruption|the corruption]], rather than an explanation of its source.
