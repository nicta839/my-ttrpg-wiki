Gill is an investigator in [[settings/Godshand/Organizations/The City Guard|Godshand's city guard]], working alongside [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Verick|Verick]] and [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Arti|Arti]] in [[settings/Godshand/Campaign/Quests/CSI godshand|CSI Godshand]].

## Confrontation at the apothecary

When the investigators confront [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Marla Greenleaf|Marla Greenleaf]] about [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Aldrich|Aldrich]], her recipes, and [[settings/Godshand/Campaign/Rumors/(K)Night's Mercy|(K)Night's Mercy]], she panics and attacks with druidic magic. Blights rise from the herbs during the confrontation.

Gill knocks Marla unconscious. The investigators examine her notes and bring both her and the notes back to the guard station. The account records her detention, without establishing a final verdict in the murder case.
