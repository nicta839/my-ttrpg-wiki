# Godshand

Godshand is a town that is an oasis within a [[Desert]]. Named as such for its noticeable landmark structure in the [[Shape of a Hand]] whose digits reach for the sky. Many people call the place a place of worship and come to perform a pilgrimmage to the temble that sits at the top of the Godshand.

The town sits near a lake, that provides enough water through aquifers to be able to fertilize the land around the town and sustain its population.

You can explore the current known [[History]] and [[settings/Godshand/Timeline|Timeline]] or maybe you're more interested in the [[Culture]].
