Godshand is an oasis town in a hostile [[settings/Godshand/Regions/Desert|desert]], named after the nearby [[settings/Godshand/Regions/The Hand Mountain and Pilgrim Stair|hand-shaped mountain]]. Access to water made it an important stop on a trading route; pilgrimage and religious tourism then helped it grow.

## Water and pilgrimage

Water falls from the Hand into [[settings/Godshand/Regions/The Lake and Waterfall|a lake]] below, although the source is unexplained. Stairs carved into the mountain lead pilgrims to a temple at its summit. Other temples stand at the bottom, where competing religious groups claim the landmark for their own divinity.

## Town life

Places mentioned in the campaign include [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Aldrich|Aldrich]]'s inn beside the brewery, [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Tibin Quickstep|Tibin Quickstep]]'s clothes shop, a blacksmith, a flower shop, and a bakery near [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Hilda|Hilda Mirin]]'s home. The [[settings/Godshand/Organizations/The Apothecary Cooperative|apothecary cooperative]] sells medicines, and the [[settings/Godshand/Organizations/The City Guard|city guard]] operates from a station.

The magistrate and councilpeople are involved in selecting the judges for [[settings/Godshand/Society/The Hand Debate|the Hand Debate]]. [[settings/Godshand/Factions/Thieves Guild|A thieves' guild]] also has a presence in town, with [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Heron|Heron]] an important figure or possible leader.

## The festival season

The yearly Debate brings religious arguments, miraculous healing, visitors, and trade. The campaign shows both its commercial opportunities and its pressures: gambling, leaked judge selections, an innkeeper's debts, and a missing corn shipment from [[settings/Godshand/Settlements/The McReady Farm|a nearby farm]].

## Surrounding dangers

The desert contains bandits and aggressive wildlife, including burrowing ants and worms. Closer to town, [[settings/Godshand/Mysteries/Anti-magic fields|anti-magic fields]], [[settings/Godshand/Mysteries/Strange metal shards in the ground|strange metal in cultivated fields]], and changes associated with [[settings/Godshand/Campaign/Rumors/Eclipse|eclipses]] remain unexplained.
