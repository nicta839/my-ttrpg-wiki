The McReady farm is a corn farm near [[settings/Godshand/Settlements/Godshand - The Town|Godshand]]. When it stops sending corn, [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Janos|Janos]] asks the party to investigate. Its fate is recorded in [[settings/Godshand/Campaign/Quests/The Great Popcorn disappearance|The Great Popcorn Disappearance]].

## People and places

Maude and Diane McReady and their twin daughters are mentioned in the account. The farm includes a cornfield, farmhouse, silo, barn, storm cellar beneath the kitchen, and a tunnel dug by the pig Ruffles. The notes later describe Maude and Diane as apparently dead; the twins are found alive in the cellar.

## The farmhouse

The party reaches a quiet farmhouse and silo after seeing animated corpses move blackened corn. Cherryblossom, [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Crimp Padfoot|Crimp]]'s ox, breaks through a locked entrance. A welcoming Mrs. McReady appears, but [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/Peter Copperpot|Peter]] hears breathing beyond the kitchen and sees through the dining area's illusion.

An altar bears [[settings/Godshand/Cosmology/The Jaded One|the Jaded One]]'s symbol upside down. Mentioning the daughters wakes Diane from a trance, and she says she locked the twins in the cellar. The children report that Ruffles changed after eating corn from the field.

## Corruption and ritual

The party follows Ruffles' tunnel into [[settings/Godshand/Mysteries/Black Goo and Cornfield Corruption|black goo and altered animals]], frees the pig from controlling crystals, and discovers a strange metal corn object. [[settings/Godshand/NPCs/RoBeert|RoBeert]]'s vision points them toward a cornfield ritual and a possible connection to [[settings/Godshand/Regions/The Lake and Waterfall|the lake and mountain]].

They weaken the ritual, defeat the summoned shadow demon, and return to town with the corn shipment for [[settings/Godshand/Society/The Hand Debate|the Debate]]. The long-term condition of the farm and the safety of the black corn are not resolved in the account.
