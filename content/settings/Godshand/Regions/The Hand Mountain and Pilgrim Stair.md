The Hand is the mountain landmark that gives [[settings/Godshand/Settlements/Godshand - The Town|Godshand]] its name. Its fingers reach toward the sky, and water falls from it into [[settings/Godshand/Regions/The Lake and Waterfall|the lake]] below.

## The pilgrim route

Stairs have been carved into the mountain so pilgrims can climb to a temple at the summit. Temples also stand at the mountain's base. Rival worshippers claim the Hand for different gods, and those competing claims help drive [[settings/Godshand/Society/The Hand Debate|the town's yearly religious festival]].

The notes establish the stair and summit temple, but do not identify the temple's custodians, its dedication, or the rules for access.

## The changing fingers

The pointer finger grows unnaturally longer over time. During predictable [[settings/Godshand/Campaign/Rumors/Eclipse|eclipses]], the sun seems to sit in the Hand and the pointer digit breaks off before beginning to grow again. Changes in the town are reported around these events.

The [[settings/Godshand/Campaign/Rumors/Shape of a Hand|rumors surrounding the Hand]] distinguish observations from speculation. Its resemblance to a divine body part, the origin of the water, and the presence of [[settings/Godshand/Mysteries/Anti-magic fields|anti-magic fields]] do not yet have established explanations.
