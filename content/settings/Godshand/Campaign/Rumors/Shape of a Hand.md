The shape of this mountain has led to many rumors springing up. Some of the observations are true, some are pure speculation.

What is known to be true is that the pointer finger of the hand seems to grow unnaturally longer as time passes. The water source from the hand seems to come from nowhere. There are naturally occurring [[settings/Godshand/Mysteries/Anti-magic fields|Anti-magic fields]] around whose origin is unknown.

What is less understandable and is source of many rumors and speculation is that during predictable [[settings/Godshand/Campaign/Rumors/Eclipse|Eclipse]]s, the pointer digit breaks off to restart its growth, something in the town seems to change.
